Nothing is stored

  • Your file is read as a stream and converted chunk by chunk as it arrives. The result streams straight back into your downloads.
  • There is no temporary file, no database and no copy. Logs hold the method, path, status and timing of each request, never names or contents.
  • To show progress, the server keeps a few counters under a random id your browser made up, and forgets them five minutes after the conversion.
  • The page loads nothing from anywhere else. Its security policy forbids it from contacting any other site.
  • Don't take our word for it: the source is open, and the same app runs on your own computer.

What is in a SAP binary export

A zip of per-shard zips holding DATA.N.BIN files, plus a DATA.0.TXT describing every field. There is no delimiter and no header. Text is UTF-16 big-endian and amounts are packed decimal (COMP-3).

The trap: a BSIS record's fields add up to 125 bytes, but records sit at 126. SAP pads odd-length records by one byte so the UTF-16 text stays aligned. Miss that and every record after the first is shifted, and the output still looks plausible. sap-bin applies the rule, checks every packed-decimal sign, and refuses to write garbage.
